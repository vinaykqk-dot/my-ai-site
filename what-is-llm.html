What is an LLM?

LLM stands for Large Language Model.

An LLM is trained on large amounts of text and can understand and generate human language.

Examples

  • ChatGPT
  • Claude
  • Gemini
  • Llama

Use Cases

  • Content Generation
  • Coding Assistance
  • Question Answering
  • Chatbots

Frequently Asked Questions

What is AI?

AI allows machines to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence.

Why is AI important?

AI improves automation and productivity.
