What is AI?

Artificial Intelligence (AI) enables computers to perform tasks such as learning, reasoning, language understanding and decision making.

What is Machine Learning?

Machine Learning is a subset of AI where systems learn patterns from data and make predictions.

What is an LLM?

Large Language Models such as ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini are trained on huge amounts of text to understand and generate human language.

What is RAG?

Retrieval Augmented Generation combines search and LLMs so responses can include current and domain-specific information.

What is Agentic AI?

Agentic AI refers to AI systems that can plan, reason and execute multiple actions to achieve goals.
