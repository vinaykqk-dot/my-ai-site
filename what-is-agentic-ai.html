What is Agentic AI?

Agentic AI refers to AI systems that can plan tasks, make decisions, and execute actions to achieve goals.

Traditional AI

Answers questions only.

Agentic AI

Plans, reasons, decides, and performs actions automatically.

Example

"Book my trip to Hyderabad" may involve searching flights, comparing options, and generating an itinerary.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is AI?
