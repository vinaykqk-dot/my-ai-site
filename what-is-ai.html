What is Artificial Intelligence (AI)?

Definition

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is the ability of computers and software to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence.

Examples of AI

  • ChatGPT
  • Microsoft Copilot
  • Google Gemini
  • Self-driving cars
  • Face recognition

Why AI Matters

Applications of AI

  • Chatbots and Virtual Assistants
  • Healthcare Diagnosis
  • Fraud Detection
  • Recommendation Systems
  • Autonomous Vehicles

AI helps automate tasks, improve productivity, and provide intelligent recommendations.
